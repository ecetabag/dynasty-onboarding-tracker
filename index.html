New here? Here's how this dashboard works (plain-English version)
1
The checklist. Every client comes from one Google Sheet, the "Onboarding Matrix." As the team collects documents, drafts the trust, gets it notarized, etc., they check off boxes on that sheet. This dashboard just reads that sheet — it never edits it.
2
What counts as "Live." A client isn't Live/Funded just because their trust is signed and notarized — money actually has to move. We check three places for proof: a separate tab that already tracks funded clients, a "Funding Completed" message in the #onboarding-notifications Slack channel, or an "STA" (stock transfer agreement) message in #trust-funding. If we only see a notary session finished, that's not enough on its own.
3
Where sign-up dates come from. The Onboarding Matrix sheet doesn't record when someone signed up, so we cross-check the #qsbs‑‑purchases Slack channel, where a payment bot posts every new sign-up with a date and email. That's what powers the Cohorts and Monthly Stage Breakdown tabs.
4
How Slack messages get matched to sheet rows. There's no shared ID between the sheet and Slack, so matching is done by name (cleaned up — lowercase, no punctuation, "Inc"/"LLC" ignored). Anything uncertain lands in the Manual Review tab instead of being guessed automatically.
5
What "Stalled" means. A client with no recorded activity for 7+ days who isn't Live yet and hasn't been marked cancelled.
6
Keeping it current. Click "Refresh Slack now" any time to pull the newest Slack messages live. The sheet data updates whenever this dashboard is rebuilt.
7
Your data is safe. This dashboard only ever reads from the Sheet and Slack — it never writes back to either one.
